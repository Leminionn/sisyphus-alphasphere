The New Designer is OptiSigns' new design editor, and our most feature-rich app: you can build complete signs, from a quick announcement to a data-driven menu board, right inside the OptiSigns portal. This guide walks through every part of it.

## Opening the New Designer

To open the New Designer, click **Files/Assets**, then **Apps**.

Next, select **Designer**. This opens the New Designer.

### The New Designer at a Glance

The New Designer is broken into 5 main zones:

* **Header** - Your design's name, plus Save, Preview, Push to Screens and Close.
* **Top Menu** - The basic functions for adding to and changing your design.
* **Canvas** - Where your design lives.
* **Bottom Toolbar** - Zoom, Layers, History and the view controls.
* **Side Menu** - Templates, Widgets, Photos, DataSources and the other additional functions.

We'll be going over each in turn.

### Canvas

The canvas is where your design lives. Each element is visible and selectable within the window.

When you select an element, a small bar appears above it with **Lock**, **Duplicate**, **Delete** and **More** options:

Clicking **More** opens a menu with additional actions, including **Cut**, **Copy**, **Copy Style**, **Paste**, **Duplicate**, **Add Component**, **Delete**, **Layer**, **Align Elements**, **Link** and **Animate**:

Elements can be moved, resized, rotated, deleted, and more from here.

## Header - Name, Save and Share

The Header runs across the top of the New Designer. It holds your design's name, the **Move**, **New Design** and **Download** buttons, and the **Preview**, **Push to Screens**, **Save** and **Close** buttons.

### Naming Your Design

By default, each new design will be titled "Your new untitled design," as shown below.

We recommend changing this name as soon as possible. While it is possible to save a design with the default name, it will be hard to identify in your Assets folder. Click the name to rename it.

### Move, New Design, and Download Functions

The **Move**, **New Design**, and **Download** functions are to the right of the name.

* **Move** - Lets you move the Design asset to a different folder. When clicked, a **Select Folder** window will appear. You can search your folders, or create a **New Folder** here if needed.
* **New Design** - Starts a new design. The **New Design** panel will appear on the Side Menu, where you can choose the new design's **Orientation** (**Landscape** or **Portrait**) and **Size**, then click **Create**.
* **Download** - Downloads the current design to your local device. You'll be able to set the **File Name** and choose between **PNG** and **JPEG** formats.

### Save

The **Save** button saves your Design asset. A message confirms that **Your Design is saved**.

The cloud icon next to the design's name shows whether all of your changes have been saved.

### Preview and Push to Screens

These options allow you to **Preview** what the design will look like when displayed on a screen, or immediately **Push to Screens**.

#### Preview

The **Preview** button will show what your design will look like if pushed to a screen.

Please note that this **Preview** may be slightly different than what will actually appear, depending on the hardware you are using to run OptiSigns and various other factors.

#### Push to Screens

Once your design has been saved, the **Push to Screens** button opens the **Push To Screens** window:

Here, you can choose a **Target** and the **Screens** to push to, set the **Scale**, and choose **Push Now**, **Schedule**, or **Temporarily**. For more information, see our article on [Pushing Contents to Screens](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18988049363859-Push-Contents-to-your-Screens).

### Close

Hitting the **Close** button will close the design. If this button is clicked before the design has been saved, the following popup will appear:

This gives you the option to **Keep Editing**, **Discard Changes**, or **Save Changes** and close.

## Top Menu - Basic Functions

The Top Menu is where basic functions can be found. These include adding images, text, shapes, backgrounds, frames, filters and animation, drawing, and resizing your design.

When you select an element on the canvas, the Top Menu changes to show the options for that element:

### Image

The **Image** option allows you to add images to your design. When clicked, it will give you two options: **My Device** and **Image Assets**.

Clicking **My Device** will let you upload an image from the device you're using, while **Image Assets** lets you add an image directly from your Files/Assets library:

When an image is selected, the Top Menu shows the image options, including **Filter**, **Crop**, **Mask**, **Background Remover**, **Flip** and **Replace**:

### Text

The **Text** option allows you to insert text into the design. Text can be chosen from either the Top Menu or the Side Menu. Either will open the **Text** panel on the Side Menu:

Clicking **Add a text box**, one of the **Predefined texts**, or one of the **Text combinations** will add text to your design.

Double-click the text to edit it, then type directly:

When the text is selected, the Top Menu shows the text options, including the font, **Font size**, color, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough**, **Text Case**, **Alignment** and **Spacing**.

Drag a handle to resize the text box. The size is shown as you drag:

Lastly, clicking the text, then holding down the mouse button and dragging allows you to reposition it.

### Shapes

The **Shapes** option allows you to add premade shapes or elements to the design. Choosing **Shapes** from the Top Menu will open the **Elements** panel on the Side Menu:

Here, you can choose between **Shapes** and **Elements** to add to the design. Clicking one will add it to the design, and the Top Menu will change to show its options:

Shapes can be resized or rotated at will. Drag a corner to resize, or drag the rotate handle below the shape to rotate it. The size and angle are shown as you drag:

### Background

The **Background** option allows you to change the background color of your design. Clicking it will open the **Canvas Background** panel on the Side Menu:

Selecting any of the colors will apply it to your design:

If none of the default colors meet your needs, you can use the color picker, type a color code, or use the **Eyedropper** to pick a color. Colors you have already used are listed under **Used in this design** and **Recent**.

### Frame

Selecting the **Frame** option will allow you to add a frame around your design. Clicking it will open the **Frame** panel on the Side Menu:

Clicking one of these frames will apply it to your design. Use the **Frame size** slider to make it thicker or thinner:

### Filter

Selecting the **Filter** option will allow you to apply one of several filters. Clicking it will open the **Filter** panel on the Side Menu:

With an image selected, clicking a filter applies it to that image. The **Adjust** section has sliders for **Exposure**, **Highlights** and **Shadows**:

### Draw

Selecting the **Draw** option will allow freeform drawing on your design. Clicking it opens the **Draw** panel on the Side Menu:

Here, you'll have a few options:

* **Stop Drawing** - Leaves the Draw panel open, but stops drawing so you can adjust the design. When clicked, this will change to **Start Drawing**.
* **Brush Type** - Selects which type of brush to use when drawing: **Pencil**, **Spray**, **Circle**, **Diamond**, **Vertical line**, **Horizontal line** or **Square**.
* **Brush Size** - Lets you customize the size of your brush.
* **Brush Color** - Select a brush color from a set of default colors or through the color picker.

Once the options you want have been selected, you can draw whatever you like on the design:

These drawn elements can be selected and edited just like any other element of the design.

### Resize

Selecting the **Resize** option allows you to change the size of the design. Clicking it will open the **Resize** panel on the Side Menu:

Here, you'll have a few options:

* **Scale design** / **Canvas only** - **Scale design** resizes everything on the canvas to match the new size. **Canvas only** changes the size of the canvas without changing the elements in the design.
* **Presets** - Common sizes, grouped under **Screen**, **Social** and **Print**, such as **FHD Landscape** (1920 x 1080), **FHD Portrait** (1080 x 1920) and **4K Landscape** (3840 x 2160).
* **Custom size** - Enter your own **Width** and **Height**. Use the lock to keep the aspect ratio.

Once you've made the adjustments you'd like, hit the **Resize** button and your design will be resized.

### Animate

Selecting the **Animate** option will allow you to add animations to your design. Clicking it will open the **Animate** panel on the Side Menu:

The **Page** tab animates the whole design, and the **Element** tab animates the selected element. To animate an element, select it and pick an animation. It plays on the canvas straight away, and the element is listed under **Element Animations**. Click **Play** to watch it again, **Add** to give the element another animation, or **Play All** to play every animation in the design:

### Undo/Redo

The **Undo** option undoes the last action. If this button is pushed in error, the **Redo** button will fix it.

|  |
| --- |
| **NOTE** |
| Undo can also be performed with **Ctrl+Z** on Windows or **Cmd+Z** on a Mac. Redo can be performed with **Ctrl+Shift+Z** or **Ctrl+Y** on Windows, or **Cmd+Shift+Z** or **Cmd+Y** on a Mac. |

### Editing Images and Elements

Selecting an image or element gives you more ways to change it from the Top Menu.

#### Crop

With an image selected, **Crop** lets you trim it. Drag the image to reposition it inside the frame, drag the corners to resize the frame, or pick an aspect ratio such as **1:1**, **16:9** or **9:16**. **Straighten** rotates the image in small steps. Press **Enter** to keep the crop, or **Esc** to cancel it.

#### Mask

**Mask** fits an image into a shape, such as a **Circle**, **Star**, **Heart** or **Hexagon**. Use **Edit Mask** to adjust it, or **Invert** to reverse it.

#### Background Remover

Select a photo, then click **Background Remover** to remove its background. Review the result, then click **Apply** to keep it or **Cancel** to leave the photo as it was.

#### Effects

**Effects** opens the **Effects** panel, where you can change an element's **Background**, **Opacity**, **Blend mode**, **Outline**, **Shadow**, **Gradient** and **Texture**.

#### Lock and Group

**Lock** keeps an element in place so it can't be moved or changed by accident. Click **Unlock** to edit it again.

To group elements, select more than one, then click **Group**. Grouped elements move and resize together. Click **Ungroup** to separate them again.

## Bottom Toolbar - Positioning and View

The Bottom Toolbar holds the **Zoom** controls, **Fit to screen**, **Layers**, **History** and **Pan**, plus the design grid, rulers, snapping and **View settings**.

### Zoom

Drag the zoom slider to zoom in or out. Click the percentage to choose a set zoom level, or **Fit**, **Fill** or **Custom**. **Fit to screen** fits the whole design in the window.

### Layers

The **Layers** button allows you to change the layering of the elements on your design. When clicked, it will open the **Position** panel on the Side Menu, under the **Layers** tab.

Here, the elements of your design are listed from the top layer to the bottom layer. Drag them by their handles to rearrange the layers:

### History

Selecting the **History** button will allow you to view the history of changes made to the design. Clicking it will open the **Position** panel on the Side Menu, under the **History** tab:

This lists each change, starting from **Initial**, the design as it was when it first opened. Clicking an entry will bring the design back to that point:

### Pan

Selecting **Pan** will allow you to move the entire view of the design, as opposed to just individual elements of it. This is most useful when you're zoomed in. You can also hold down the **Space** bar to pan temporarily.

### Grid, Rulers, and Snapping

The next three buttons turn the **design grid**, the **rulers**, and **snapping** on or off. Snapping lines elements up with the grid, guides, other elements, and the edges and center of the page as you move them.

### View Settings

**View settings** lets you fine-tune these tools: the grid size, rulers and guides, what elements **Snap to**, **Safe areas** for screens that crop the edges, and a **Legibility check** that flags text too small to read at a set screen size and viewing distance.

## Side Menu - Additional Functions

The **Side Menu** is where most of the additional functions for the New Designer live.

Each item opens its own panel next to the Side Menu. Together, they turn a simple, static design into dynamic signage.

Click the arrow on the edge of the panel to collapse it and give the canvas more room:

### Templates

**Templates** opens the OptiSigns template library, grouped into collections such as **Featured**, **Popular**, **Top 20 Promo Designs**, **Holidays and Observances** and **Seasonal Promotions**. Click **Show all** to see a whole collection, or search for a template by name.

Click a template to load it onto the canvas. Every part of it can then be edited like the rest of your design:

### Your Templates

**Your Templates** holds the templates and components your team has saved, on the **Templates** and **Components** tabs. Click **Add Current Design as Template** to add a copy of the design you're working on to your team's template library.

### Widgets

**Widgets** add live content to your design, such as **Overlay Image**, **Video**, **Document**, **Apps**, **Playlist**, **Time**, **Weather**, **Date**, **Scrolling Text** and **Calendar**. Click a widget to add it, then set it up in the panel that opens.

### Photos and GIPHY

**Photos** lets you search a library of free stock photos from Unsplash. Click a photo to add it to your design.

**GIPHY** adds animated **Stickers**, **GIFs**, **Emoji** and **Text** from GIPHY. Search for what you need, then click it to add it.

### Text and Elements

**Text** opens the same **Text** panel as the Top Menu, with **Add a text box**, the **Predefined texts**, ready-made **Text combinations** and **Browse all fonts**.

**Elements** opens the shapes and icons you can add to your design, under **Shapes** and **Elements**. Search to find a specific icon.

### QR Code

Click **QR Code**, then **Add QR Code**. A QR code is added to your design, and the panel shows what it links to. Choose a type, such as **Website URL**, **Email**, **Call**, **SMS**, **Wifi**, **WhatsApp** or **Asset**, enter the details, then click **Update QR Code**.

### DataSources and Repeaters

A **DataSource** connects your design to data that changes, such as a spreadsheet, so what's on the screen updates without you editing the design. Click **DataSource** to see the data sources used in this design and your other data sources, then click **Add DataSource** to connect a new one.

You can create a table yourself, or connect **Google Sheets**, **Microsoft 365 Excel**, a **REST API**, an **XML or JSON URL**, an **API Gateway** or an **Inbound Webhook**, or paste data from your clipboard:

A **Repeater** shows a list from a data source, with the same layout repeated for each row, such as a team directory or a birthday list. Click **Repeaters**, then **Add Blank Repeater** to build your own, or start from one of the ready-made **Repeater Components**, such as **Birthday**, **Meet the Team**, **Building** and **Menu** components.

### Brand Kit

**Brand Kit** keeps your designs on-brand. Upload your own fonts under **Font Management**, then add your **Brand Fonts** and **Brand Colors**.

**Enforce Branding** sets how strictly the brand is applied:

* **None** - Every font and color is available as usual.
* **Guideline** - Your brand fonts and colors are shown first, but nothing is removed.
* **Enforce** - Only your brand fonts and colors can be used.
