# Android TV / ChromeCast

Turn any Android TV, Google TV or Chromecast with Google TV into a digital sign. You install the free OptiSigns Digital Signage app from the Google Play Store, pair it with your OptiSigns account, and then manage what it shows from the OptiSigns portal.

---

## Install the OptiSigns App

On your TV's home screen, open the Google Play Store. On Android TV, select **Get more apps**.

Search for **OptiSigns Digital Signage** and select it in the results.

Select **Install**.

When the installation finishes, open the app.

The app shows a pairing code. Keep it on screen; you'll need it in the next section.

---

## Pair the TV with Your Account

1. On a computer or phone, go to [app.optisigns.com](https://app.optisigns.com/) and log in. If you don't have an account yet, select **Create Account**. You can also log in with Google, Microsoft or Facebook.

2. On the **Screens** page, select **Add Screen**.

3. Enter the pairing code shown on your TV in **Pair code**, then select **Pair**.

**Note:** Codes are case-sensitive. The portal enters them in capitals. If the code on your TV has lowercase letters, select **Use lowercase pairing code** first.

The TV changes to show that it's paired.

---

## Start OptiSigns Automatically

The app's **Autostart** setting is on by default, so OptiSigns opens by itself when the TV turns on. If your TV doesn't allow apps to start on their own, the app shows a message saying so. In that case, open OptiSigns with the remote each time the TV restarts.

**Tip:** On newer Android TV versions, apps need the **Display over other apps** permission to start on their own. If OptiSigns doesn't open after a restart, allow this permission for OptiSigns in your TV's app settings.
